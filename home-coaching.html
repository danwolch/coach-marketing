Frequently asked questions

Why not just ask an AI?

Use one. Everyone you're competing with does, which is the problem. A general AI hasn't heard your interviews or your 1:1s, it gives the next person the same advice it gave you, and it tends to agree with whatever you push it toward.

A coach who has heard your real calls can tell you the one thing to fix this week, and you'll feel accountable to them in a way you won't to an app.

Is the coach a real person?

Yes. On Coach + Review, a coach reviews your real conversations and writes to you every month. On Coach + Live, you also meet live each month. If you already work with a coach, bring them.

I already have a coach. Can I use Work Coach with them?

Yes. Ask your coach for their Work Coach link. If they aren't set up yet, send them to the page for coaches. Once you're connected, you choose what to share.

What does my coach see?

Only what you share, one conversation or practice session at a time. You can stop sharing anything later, and if you stop working with them, everything you shared goes with you.

Does a bot join my calls?

No. Work Coach records on your own computer. No bot participant joins the call. As with any recording tool, you're responsible for following the recording laws where you live and your employer's policies.

I have a job. Will my employer see any of this?

No. Not your employer. Not other users. Your recordings and transcripts are yours. Data isn't used to train shared models. You can delete everything anytime.

Can I use the app without a coach?

Yes. The $50 plan is the app on its own: it reviews your calls, shows you the moments, and lets you practice. You can add a coach whenever you want one.

What does it cost?

Plans start at $50/month for the app, $250/month to add human review, and $500/month for live coaching sessions. Every plan starts with a 2-week free trial with no credit card required.

I'm a coach. Is this trying to replace me?

No. Looking for a job has turned into an arms race, and so has doing well at work. People feel far more accountable to a person than to an AI, and human judgment is still what helps them most.
