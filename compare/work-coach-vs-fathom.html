Work Coach vs Fathom

Fathom is one of the strongest free meeting notetakers. Work Coach charges for a different result: private feedback on how you handled the meeting.

11 min read · Updated July 2026

The short answer

Pick Fathom if you want recordings, transcripts, and useful summaries at the lowest possible price. Its free plan includes unlimited meetings, transcription, and storage. The first five calls each month get advanced summaries, while later calls get a simpler chronological summary.

Pick Work Coach if the meeting itself is a performance you want to improve. Interviews, 1:1s, presentations, and difficult conversations benefit from timestamped coaching, filler-word playback, voice delivery analysis, and practice. Fathom's team scorecards are built for managers reviewing a group, while Work Coach's feedback belongs only to you.

For many people the sensible answer is both: Fathom handles the record and Work Coach handles the private debrief.

What Fathom does

Fathom records Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams calls, then produces a transcript, summary, action items, clips, and follow-up email drafts. Its free plan is the headline: unlimited recordings, unlimited transcription, and unlimited storage. Advanced AI templates, Ask Fathom, automatic action items, and custom summaries are available for the first five calls each month on Free and without that cap on Premium.

Capture is in transition. The established experience uses a visible Fathom notetaker in the meeting. A new desktop experience rolling out in 2026 adds bot-free capture, with Zoom bot-free video already available on Mac and broader Meet and Teams support still being expanded in July. Fathom deliberately keeps a consent signal in either mode: a visible participant, a recording banner, an in-chat notice, or an in-app consent prompt.

Fathom also has a serious team product. Team Edition adds shared call libraries, CRM sync, comments, keyword alerts, and folders. The top Team plan adds AI scorecards and behavioral metrics for managers. That coaching is tied to team oversight rather than a worker-owned private account.

What Work Coach does

Work Coach is a desktop app for Mac and Windows that records microphone and system audio locally. Nothing joins the call, and recording starts only after you choose it. The meeting can be in Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Slack, or any other app that plays audio through the computer.

You get a timestamped transcript, an editable summary, and action items. The main product begins after those basics:

  • Role-aware feedback. Work Coach identifies the meeting type and your role, then applies the right standard. An interview answer is judged differently from a manager's 1:1 or a seller's discovery call.
  • Evidence you can replay. Coaching points link to the exact moment in the conversation instead of relying on generic advice.
  • Filler-word feedback. It counts your fillers per minute and lets you listen to every instance in sequence.
  • Voice delivery analysis. High-stakes clips are reviewed for pace, pitch, pauses, projection, and tone.
  • Practice from real meetings. Voice roleplays let you retry difficult conversations and track whether the next attempt improves.

There is no employer dashboard, team workspace, or admin account. The person being coached owns the account and keeps the history when they change jobs.

How recording works

Fathom's recording story now has two branches. The familiar branch sends a notetaker into the call, where it can be admitted, removed, renamed on paid plans, and seen by participants. The newer branch captures from the desktop without adding a participant. As of July 2026 that bot-free experience is still rolling out, with platform details varying by operating system and meeting app.

Fathom does not promise silent recording. Its consent documentation says every capture path keeps some form of notice. Work Coach does not notify the room on your behalf. It asks you to start locally and leaves disclosure to you, so you must follow company policy and the law where everyone is located.

Fathom wins if you need video, clips, or a recording participants can revisit. Work Coach stores no cloud audio and concentrates on your side of the conversation.

What happens after the meeting

Fathom's output is designed to move work forward. It can draft a follow-up email, extract action items, create different summary formats, answer questions about the call, cut shareable clips, and sync notes into Salesforce, HubSpot, Close, Slack, or Zapier. That saves real administrative time.

Work Coach spends its processing budget elsewhere. It can show that an answer wandered before reaching the point, attach the exact clip, explain which meeting skill was weak, and generate a practice conversation. Fathom's AI scorecards can help a sales manager review a team, but the manager owns that workflow. Work Coach exists for a person who wants candid feedback without creating a performance record at work.

Privacy and who owns the output

Fathom is SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, and GDPR compliant. It stores data in the United States. Its AI subprocessors are contractually barred from training on meeting content. Fathom says it may use de-identified data to improve proprietary models, with an opt-out in settings. Deleting an account removes recording data and metadata, then clears backups after seven additional days. Details are in Fathom's security guide.

Workspace ownership matters. When you join a Fathom Team account, the account and its content become managed by that team. Admins can enforce visibility for future external meetings. That is useful governance for a company and a poor fit for coaching notes you want to carry to your next employer.

Work Coach keeps the product boundary simple. Meeting audio remains on your computer and is deleted automatically after 30 days. No audio file is stored in the cloud. Transcripts and coaching live in your private account, are never visible to your employer, and are never used to train shared models. There is no company administrator who can turn that promise into a workspace setting later.

Pricing

Fathom Free costs $0 and keeps unlimited recordings, storage, and transcription. It supplies advanced summaries for five calls per month, then falls back to a chronological summary. Premium is $15 per user per month on annual billing and removes the advanced AI cap. Team Edition is $19 per seat annually, and Team Edition Pro is $29, adding deal views, coaching metrics, SSO, retention controls, and advanced security. See the current matrix on Fathom's pricing page.

If notes are the only requirement, Work Coach cannot compete with free. Its value has to come from changing what you do in the next meeting.

Work Coach costs $50 a month after a two-week free trial with no credit card. Plans at $250 and $500 add human review or a live monthly coaching session. That is expensive beside a basic notetaker and inexpensive beside a traditional executive coach, which commonly costs around $5,000 a month. The purchase only makes sense if feedback and practice are the reason you record.

Can you use both together?

Yes. Let Fathom create the shareable record while Work Coach records locally for your private review. If you prefer not to run two recorders, Work Coach can import a transcript afterward. Keep the purposes clear: the Fathom output can belong to a team, while the coaching should stay in your personal account.

Which one should you pick?

Pick Fathom if you...

  • Want unlimited free recording and transcription
  • Need video, clips, follow-up emails, or CRM sync
  • Want a shared team call library
  • Prefer a recorder that provides an explicit consent signal

Pick Work Coach if you...

  • Want feedback on your performance, not a team scorecard
  • Need every coaching point kept away from your employer
  • Want voice delivery analysis and practice
  • Use many meeting apps and want one local capture method

Frequently asked questions

Is Fathom really free?

Yes. Fathom Free includes unlimited recordings, transcription, and storage. Advanced summaries and related AI features apply to the first five calls each month, after which calls receive a simpler chronological summary.

Can Fathom record without a bot?

For eligible users, yes. Fathom's newer desktop experience supports bot-free capture, but the rollout and video support vary by platform. Fathom still provides a consent signal through chat, banners, prompts, or the visible notetaker.

Does Fathom coach speakers?

Its top Team plan includes AI scorecards and behavioral metrics for team managers. It does not provide Work Coach's worker-owned role-aware feedback, voice delivery review, or private practice mode.

Who owns Fathom meetings in a team account?

Fathom says that after a user joins a Team account, the account and content are managed by the team. Admins can enforce sharing of future external meetings.

Can Fathom and Work Coach run together?

Yes. Fathom can create the team record while Work Coach provides a private coaching pass. Work Coach can also import a transcript instead of recording the meeting again.
