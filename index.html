The feedback gap

Everyone sees how you could grow. Nobody tells you

Nobody's out to get you. Telling you the hard stuff is awkward and risky, so most people just don't. The feedback that could change your career is sitting in other people's heads. You just never hear it.

The recruiter

Knows exactly why you didn't get the offer. Telling you carries risk and zero upside, so all you hear is "we went in another direction."

Your peers

See your blind spots every day. They also like you, and honesty feels like hurting a friend, so they keep it to themselves.

Your manager

Walks a tightrope between honest and encouraging. Real talk about what's between you and the promotion is uncomfortable, so it waits for review season, softened.

Your team

If you're the manager, nobody tells the boss the hard truth. Feedback almost never flows up.

None of them are wrong. You're just not getting the information you need to succeed. Work Coach fills the gap with honest, private feedback from your real meetings, so you can become the person they already see you could be.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to get started?

Join the waitlist and tell us your goal. Once you're in, you download the Mac app and your coach starts learning from your meetings. Every plan begins with a 2-week free trial with no credit card required.

Is there a real human coach?

Yes, that's your choice. The base plan ($50/mo) is your always-on AI coach. Add human review of your progress ($250/mo), or a live monthly session with a real work coach ($500/mo). You can change tiers anytime.

Does a bot join my calls?

No. Work Coach captures audio locally on your Mac. No bot participant, no awkward notification. No one in your meeting will know it's there.

Is this for job seekers?

Absolutely. Your coach helps you organize the search, sharpen your resume, prep for interviews, review how you actually performed, and negotiate the offer. It's just as useful if you're aiming for a promotion or leveling up in your current role.

Will my employer care?

Work Coach analyzes how you communicate and ignores your company's proprietary information. Your recordings and transcripts are private and never shared.

Do you share data with anyone?

No. Not your employer. Not other users. Your recordings and transcripts are yours. Data isn't used to train shared models. You can delete everything anytime.

Who is this for?

Anyone who wants to be better in professional conversations, whether you're leading a team, collaborating with peers, or interviewing for your next role.

What does it cost?

Plans start at $50/month for the AI coach, $250/month to add human review, and $500/month for live coaching sessions. Every plan starts with a 2-week free trial with no credit card required.
